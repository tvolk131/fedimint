These are fresh measurements of the bounded worker pools, submission admission
and decoded-program cache, not a reuse of the October timings. They cover a
warm, closed-loop core validation path only. They do not qualify Pi 5 hardware,
production API queueing or consensus progress under hostile submission traffic.

The harness smoke check passed before measuring. Every probe uses one fresh
process, a warm small RocksDB, 2,000 iterations per caller and asserted expected
outcomes. Two passes per cell ran sequentially, with no active Cargo/rustc or
guardian test processes at the start. No builds ran alongside the probes.
Power, cooling and thermal state were not controlled. These remain short host
observations, not sustained-load or statistical tail bounds.

The `mixed` probe alternates cap-fitting packed-constant Simplicity validation
and a real Mint v2 one-note input/output control. `mixed_33_bad_last` is the
cap-fitting constant/signature workload with a final failing signature. It
must reject every time. Separate invalid-only and valid mixed runs do not
establish simultaneous valid/invalid traffic behavior.

At 14 available CPUs the configured executor selects seven submission workers
and fourteen admission permits. Sixteen closed-loop callers can compete for
that admission; the measurements do not record queue occupancy or demonstrate
production API admission bounds.

## Observations

Ranges span the two fresh-process passes. Latencies are milliseconds; peak RSS
is the whole process lifetime high-water mark, including setup, caches, stacks
and C allocations. It is not incremental validation memory or an RSS limit.

| Workload | Callers | Class; samples per pass | p50 ms | p99 ms | Process peak MiB |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| mixed | 4 | Simplicity; 4,000 | 1.065–1.073 | 1.278–1.629 | 83.38–84.06 |
| mixed | 4 | Mint v2; 4,000 | 1.700–1.711 | 1.803–1.865 | same process |
| mixed | 16 | Simplicity; 16,000 | 2.081–2.092 | 4.936–7.006 | 85.59–86.13 |
| mixed | 16 | Mint v2; 16,000 | 1.721–1.723 | 3.935–4.386 | same process |
| late invalid signature | 16 | Simplicity; 32,000 | 9.263–9.279 | 11.953–12.455 | 85.03–85.19 |

Measured intervals were 2.78–2.81 seconds for four-caller mixed runs,
4.12–4.37 seconds for sixteen-caller mixed runs, and 18.76–18.89 seconds for
invalid-signature runs. All outcomes matched their prechecked results. Mint
made progress in the mixed runs; this is not a consensus-progress claim.

Repeated identical programs/witnesses intentionally exercise a warm decoded
cache. Unique-witness churn, cold-device behavior, arbitrary maximum-memory
shapes and sustained history growth are not covered. Cache hits are performance
only; the correctness suite separately checks commitment/version/cost rejection,
partial retention, cancellation and admission behavior.

## Reproduction and retained evidence

Use the [harness instructions](README.md) and the recorded compiler flags.
Build/smoke-test once, then run each probe in its own process twice:

```sh
cargo +1.93.0 bench --locked -p fedimint-simplicity-server --bench resources -- --test

FM_SIMPLICITY_BENCH_LOAD=mixed,4,2000,rocks cargo +1.93.0 bench --locked \
  -p fedimint-simplicity-server --bench resources
FM_SIMPLICITY_BENCH_LOAD=mixed,16,2000,rocks cargo +1.93.0 bench --locked \
  -p fedimint-simplicity-server --bench resources
FM_SIMPLICITY_BENCH_LOAD=mixed_33_bad_last,16,2000,rocks cargo +1.93.0 bench --locked \
  -p fedimint-simplicity-server --bench resources
```

The recorded run invoked the already-built executable directly after its
smoke check, avoiding compilation during measurement. Raw JSON, smoke output,
host/compiler/lockfile metadata, executable identity and fixture manifest are
retained locally under ignored `evidence/resources/`. Preserve those files if
retaining this run. Earlier 80-iteration smoke probes are separate from the
2,000-iteration results reported here.
